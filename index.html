How it works

Safe deals, with AirMilesHK in the middle

Every deal goes through us — the same way Binance P2P holds crypto in escrow. Buyers and sellers never send money to each other directly.

  1. Buyer makes an offerA bid on miles/points, the whole account, or a request for a ticket or hotel stay.
  2. Seller respondsAccept, counter with a new price, or decline. Counter as many times as you need.
  3. AirMilesHK holds paymentOnce agreed, we contact both sides. The buyer pays AirMilesHK, not the seller.
  4. Delivery, then releaseThe seller delivers. Both sides confirm, and we release the funds to the seller.
Free during launch. Joining, listing and making offers cost nothing. Read the marketplace rules before you trade. Most loyalty programmes do not allow selling or transferring miles, and you trade at your own risk.
