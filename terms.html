Marketplace rules

Rules for trading

Every member agrees to these rules when they join. Read them before you list or make an offer.

Draft, version 0.1. AirMilesHK will finalise these rules before public launch.

1. Who can trade

  • You must be 18 or over and register with your real full name, a working email and a WhatsApp number.
  • One account per person. Your public nickname is the only detail other members see.
  • Before a deal completes, AirMilesHK may ask for government ID. The name on your ID must match the name on the loyalty account (sellers) or on the payment (buyers).

2. The programme rules risk, in plain words

Most airline and hotel loyalty programmes do not allow members to sell, buy or transfer miles, points or accounts. If a programme finds out, it can cancel the miles, cancel tickets booked with them, or close the account. This is normally a breach of contract, not a crime, but the loss can be real. You trade at your own risk. AirMilesHK is not affiliated with any programme.

3. What you can list

  • Award bookings (lowest risk). You keep your account and book a flight or stay in the buyer's name. This is the option we recommend.
  • Partial balances. Miles or points moved by a method the programme itself offers, such as a points transfer or family pooling, where that method exists.
  • Whole accounts (highest risk). Allowed only with a signed written agreement between the seller, the buyer and AirMilesHK, and with ID checks on both sides. Many programmes treat this as account fraud. AirMilesHK may refuse any whole-account deal.
  • You may only list accounts in your own name that you control. No business, shared, stolen or bought-in accounts.

4. How a deal works

  1. The buyer sends an offer or a booking request. The seller accepts, counters or declines.
  2. An accepted offer is a commitment from both sides. AirMilesHK then contacts you both on WhatsApp.
  3. The buyer pays AirMilesHK, never the seller. We hold the payment.
  4. The seller delivers (transfer, ticket or booking) only after AirMilesHK confirms it has the payment.
  5. Once both sides confirm delivery, AirMilesHK releases the payment to the seller.

5. Off-platform contact is not allowed

Do not share phone numbers, emails or payment details in listings or messages, and do not try to complete a deal outside AirMilesHK. Members who do will be suspended. This rule protects both sides: without AirMilesHK in the middle, there is no protection at all.

6. Disputes and refunds

  • If the seller does not deliver, the buyer is refunded in full from the money AirMilesHK holds.
  • If a ticket or booking is cancelled by the programme before travel, AirMilesHK will decide the outcome case by case using the evidence from both sides.
  • AirMilesHK's decision on held funds is final.

7. Fees

The marketplace is free during launch. If fees are introduced later (a monthly membership or a fee per completed deal), we will announce them in advance. They will never apply to deals already agreed.

8. Your data

We store your name, email, WhatsApp number, country, listings and deal history so we can run the marketplace and arrange deals. Other members only ever see your nickname. See the AirMilesHK privacy policy.

9. Suspension

AirMilesHK can remove listings or suspend accounts that break these rules, that look fraudulent, or where a programme has flagged the account.
